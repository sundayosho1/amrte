# S2 Breakout & Volatility Expansion Strategy

S2 is an offline research strategy implemented through the Prompt 11 lifecycle and Prompt 12 centralized scorer. It emits immutable research candidates/signals or `NO_ACTION`; it has no order, account, sizing, stop, target, or execution capability.

Two independently registered variants are provided:

- `S2_BREAKOUT_VOLATILITY_EXPANSION_IMMEDIATE` qualifies after authoritative pre-breakout compression, a Prompt 6 structural break, Prompt 7 volatility expansion, and directionally consistent displacement.
- `S2_BREAKOUT_VOLATILITY_EXPANSION_RETEST` shares the same deterministic breakout-event identity, then requires Prompt 6 role-reversal zone evidence and execution-timeframe resumption displacement.

Default policy is strict: compression must be confirmed, wick-only breaks are rejected, unknown/abnormal regimes are blocked, and session/news restrictions retain precedence. Opposing confirmed structure shifts classify the event as a false break and create immutable invalidation evidence.

Timeframe roles, thresholds, variant enablement, history bounds, and research TTL are configuration-driven. Volume, financial risk/reward, and correlation are not fabricated when no authoritative owner supplies them.
