# S3 Range & Mean-Reversion Strategy

AMRTE v0.15.0 adds S3 as a Prompt 11 strategy using Prompt 12 centralized scoring. It consumes the immutable Prompt 10 intelligence package and produces research artifacts or `NO_ACTION`; it contains no brokerage, account, sizing, stop, target, exit, or execution functionality.

S3 first requires Prompt 8 RANGE compatibility and Prompt 6 two-sided consolidation evidence. A confirmed Prompt 6 break, TREND, BREAKOUT_EXPANSION, ABNORMAL, UNKNOWN, aligned directional structure, restricted session, or blocking news policy prevents qualification. RSI and Bollinger evidence cannot independently create a candidate.

The point-in-time lifecycle is deliberately sequential. An outside/edge observation records an immutable extreme. Only a later intelligence snapshot can establish re-entry and directionally consistent confirmation-timeframe displacement toward the structural midpoint. This prevents later rejection or re-entry evidence from being backdated.

`RangeEvidence`, range boundaries, mean references, setups, and `MeanReversionDestination` are immutable and deterministically identified. The destination is an explanatory research landmark—not an executable take-profit instruction. State and history are bounded and recovery metadata is compatibility-validated.

Current conservative limitations are documented in the Prompt 15 delivery report. In particular, raw price-distance tolerance remains unavailable without a richer authoritative upstream point-in-time price/boundary interaction series.
